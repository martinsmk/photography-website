What I look for in Car Photos

  1. Good lighting
  2. Clean background
  3. Interesting angles
  4. Strong colors

When taking car photos, I focus on capturing the vehicle's design, performance, and the emotions it evokes. I aim to highlight the unique features and craftsmanship that make each car special.
